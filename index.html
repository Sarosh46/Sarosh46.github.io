About

About Me

I am a third-semester Information Security student at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST). I'm learning that security is less about tools and more about thinking carefully: how systems are built, how they break, and how to protect them.

Right now I'm building my foundations through hands-on labs in encoding, hashing, disk forensics and password security, along with programming in C++ and creating websites with HTML and CSS. Every lab shows me how much of our digital world depends on trust.

I believe good security starts with understanding, not shortcuts, and I'm working towards defending systems responsibly. This website is where I share that journey.
